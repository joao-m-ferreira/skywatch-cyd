See the planes flying over your home

Put this on a small ESP32 touchscreen and it shows the aircraft nearby, with a 3D model in the airline's colours.

This needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (not a phone), because it talks to your board over USB.

Plug the board in with a USB data cable, then press Install.
