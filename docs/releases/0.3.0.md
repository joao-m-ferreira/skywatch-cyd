Third release of **skywatch-cyd**. The big change: you can now flash a board from your browser.

## Flash from your browser
Open the [web portal](https://joao-m-ferreira.github.io/skywatch-cyd/), pick your board, press **Install**, then type your WiFi name and password
into the page: it sends them to the board over USB. You need Chrome or Edge on a computer and a USB data
cable. The page asks for confirmation before installing a board nobody has tried on real hardware.

## One build per board
Each release now has a full image and an app-only update for every board in `firmware/boards.json`:

| Board | `<board>` | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ESP32-2432S028R "Cheap Yellow Display" 2.8" (ILI9341) | `cyd` | tested on real hardware |
| 2.8" CYD with two USB ports (ST7789) | `cyd-st7789` | not tested on hardware |
| 3.5" "big CYD" (ST7796) | `cyd35` | experimental |
| Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.8 | `amoled18` | emulator only |

## Also new
- Screen layouts that adapt to the panel: plane left, plane right, or plane on top with the text below
  (`set layout`), plus portrait rotations.
- Board settings over serial (`set panel`, `set invert`, `set rgb`, `set rotation`, `set led`) and a
  colour test card (`display test`) to identify your panel.
- Stability: complete HTTP bodies, a memory-fragmentation guard, the reason for the last restart in
  `status`, and a safe mode after repeated crashes.
- Better aircraft: per-family and per-type shapes, grey unpainted wings, many corrected airline liveries
  (about 165 airlines).

## Install by hand
Download `skywatch-cyd-0.3.0-<board>-merged.bin` and flash it at `0x0`
(`esptool.py --chip esp32 --port <port> write_flash 0x0 <file>`; `--chip esp32s3` for the AMOLED board),
or the `-firmware.bin` at `0x10000` to keep your settings. Verify the downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

## Good to know
- Data: adsb.fi open data (personal, non-commercial use), adsbdb routes, and optional photos (credited on screen).
- The Waveshare AMOLED build has only ever run in an emulator, never on the real board.
