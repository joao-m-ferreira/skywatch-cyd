Second release of **skywatch-cyd**: the nearby-flights display for the ESP32-2432S028 ("Cheap Yellow
Display"). This is the first release with downloadable binaries.

## What's new
- **Better paint schemes**: TAP (logo block, red fin with green wedge), easyJet (orange crown and rear),
  Air Europa, Emirates (UAE-flag fin), Etihad (faceted fin, gold title), Maersk Air Cargo, Air Transat, and
  corrections for Lufthansa, Air France, KLM, Austrian, Iberia, Vueling and more. About 25 airlines added.
- **Night mode touch-to-wake** now lasts 10 minutes, and you can change it: `set wake <minutes>`.
- **`flights`** over serial lists the aircraft currently detected, with route and airline.

## Install
Download `skywatch-cyd-0.2.0-merged.bin` and flash it at `0x0` (first install, erases saved settings),
or `skywatch-cyd-0.2.0-firmware.bin` at `0x10000` (update, keeps your settings):
```
esptool.py --chip esp32 --port <port> --baud 460800 write_flash 0x0 skywatch-cyd-0.2.0-merged.bin
```
Then open a serial terminal at **115200 baud**, type `help`, and set `set ssid` / `set pass`.
Full steps, including flashing from the browser, are in the README. Verify the downloads against `SHA256SUMS`.

## Good to know
- Built for the **ESP32-2432S028R** (resistive touch, ILI9341). Capacitive variants use other pins.
- Data: adsb.fi, adsbdb routes, planespotters.net photos (set a contact with `set contact <email>`).
- Liveries are approximations: no logos or lettering.
